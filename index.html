HOW TO PLAY
All-Time NBA Challenge
  • 🏀Start with a 12-man roster with random NBA players, and build it into a Hall of Fame team.
  • 🏆Beat 5 cups: Franchise, States, College, Continents, Decades. Clear them all to complete your career.
  • 🔍Sign Mystery Free Agents. After every game, you can sign a new player. But be patient: each win reveals one clue to know better who you're adding.
  • ✅Guess a free agent right and you'll earn a bonus teammate from that player's team/season — two signings for the price of one!
